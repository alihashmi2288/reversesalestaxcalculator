VAT (Value Added Tax) is a consumption tax assessed on goods and services in the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, New Zealand, and over 160 countries globally. In standard retail environments throughout Europe and Australasia, prices are displayed inclusive of VAT (gross price) . When businesses, freelancers, or accountants need to claim input tax credits or reconcile invoices, they must calculate the net (ex-VAT) amount and isolate the tax component.
