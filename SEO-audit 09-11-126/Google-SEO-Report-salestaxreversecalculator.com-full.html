2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 84/100

What Works

  • 100% canonical accuracy with zero mismatches or cross-domain issues across all 79 URLs
  • Clean robots.txt allowing search and AI crawlers with explicit sitemap reference
  • Solid edge performance on Vercel with 447ms average server TTFB
  • Real HTTP 404 response on non-existent endpoints (no soft-404 catch-alls)
  • Full HTTPS enforcement with Strict-Transport-Security (max-age=63072000; preload)

Findings

9 Canadian provincial pages orphaned with 0 internal links Critical

Pages for Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland, PEI, NWT, Nunavut, and Yukon exist in sitemap.xml but have zero internal inlinks.

Recommendation: Update /canada to include a full grid of all 13 provinces/territories with descriptive anchor text.

Fragment URLs accumulating impressions in Google Search Console Medium

URLs with hash fragments (/#calculator, /#faq, /#how-it-works) appear in GSC with over 140 combined impressions.

Recommendation: Audit internal navigation links and canonical references to ensure all links use clean path-based URLs without bare hash anchors.

Missing Content-Security-Policy (CSP) Header Low

Vercel edge responses include HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, and X-Frame-Options, but no Content-Security-Policy is set.

Recommendation: Configure a Content-Security-Policy header in next.config.mjs to protect users against XSS and injection.

  • High depth on state pages with local county/city tax nuance tables and exemptions
  • Clear authoritativeness signals including dedicated Person and Organization review team schemas

Findings

Boilerplate pattern across state pages risks repetitive content filtering Medium

50 state pages follow identical structural sections. While specific tax rates differ, intro and FAQ paragraphs share template phrasing.

Recommendation: Enrich top 15 highest-impression states (NY, CT, TX, UT, CA, NC, FL) with state-specific filing deadlines, nexus thresholds, and local tax nuance tables.

High impression query mismatches on specific tax math queries Medium

Users search for concrete math examples like 'christa went shopping for back-to-school clothes... if sales tax is 6% what was total' (27 imp, pos 9.4).

Recommendation: Add a 'Worked Examples & Word Problems' section to the FAQ addressing common receipt totals ($10, $50, $100, $176) at standard rates.

2.3 On-Page SEO

Score: 84/100

What Works

  • 100% single H1 tag coverage across all 79 site pages
  • Zero duplicate titles or duplicate meta descriptions detected across the entire site
  • Logical H2 and H3 heading hierarchies mirroring searcher intent
  • Consistent Open Graph and Twitter Card social metadata implementation

Findings

55 Page titles exceed 60 characters (SERP pixel truncation) High

Titles on state pages average 68-70 characters (e.g. 'Arkansas Sales Tax Calculator 2026: Calculate or Back Out Tax (9.49%)'), causing tax rates to truncate in mobile SERPs.

Recommendation: Refactor state title template to: '[State] Reverse Sales Tax Calculator ([Rate]%)' (under 55 characters).

50 Meta descriptions exceed 160 characters Medium

Descriptions reach 172-201 characters, resulting in truncated snippets that hide the call to action.

Recommendation: Tighten meta descriptions to 145-155 characters, leading with the core utility and instant pre-tax extraction.

Low CTR on #1 Impression State Page: /us/new-york (0.05% CTR) High

/us/new-york ranks at average position 9.1 with 2,133 impressions but only 1 click, indicating snippet mismatch for NYC and county searchers.

Recommendation: Update New York meta title to: 'New York Reverse Sales Tax Calculator: 8.875% NYC Included' to match high-volume local search intent.
