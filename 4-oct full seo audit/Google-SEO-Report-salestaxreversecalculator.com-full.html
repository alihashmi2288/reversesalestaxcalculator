2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 88/100

What Works

  • 100% crawlable raw HTML (7,673 words SSR without client JS requirement)
  • Valid XML sitemap with 81 URLs submitting cleanly in Google Search Console
  • Strict HSTS (preload, max-age 63072000), nosniff, and SAMEORIGIN security headers
  • Edge TTFB of 46ms via Vercel globally distributed CDN
  • Perfect 100/100 Lighthouse Best Practices and SEO scores

Findings

Sitemap & Canonical Trailing Slash Inconsistency Medium

sitemap.xml lists URLs without trailing slashes (e.g. https://salestaxreversecalculator.com), whereas the server canonical URL matches with slash.

Recommendation: Standardize all URLs in sitemap.xml to match the exact canonical URLs.

2-Hop Redirect Chain on HTTP WWW Subdomain Low

http://www.salestaxreversecalculator.com redirects to https://www. (308) then to https://root (301).

Recommendation: Add a single edge routing rule in next.config.js or Vercel config to redirect directly to https://salestaxreversecalculator.com/.

2.2 Content Quality & Scaled Content

Score: 65/100

What Works

  • Homepage contains 6,687 words of thorough educational, formulaic, and practical tax guidance
  • New York state page has been upgraded to 2,408 words with deep NYC local tax nuances
  • Clear step-by-step mathematical proofs and receipt decoding instructions

Findings

Scaled Content Boilerplate Across 49 State Pages (Primary Drop Cause) Critical

49 out of 50 state pages have ~910 words that follow a word-for-word template where only the state name is substituted. This programmatic footprint caused Google to apply a domain-level quality suppression in early September 2026.

Recommendation: Follow the NYC rewrite blueprint across the top 10 highest-traffic states (CA, TX, FL, PA, IL, OH, GA, NC, MI, NJ), adding state-specific nexus rules, local county ranges, and unique receipt examples.

Keyword Cannibalization Across /, /how-it-works, and /faq High

GSC reveals Google cycling rankings between /, /how-it-works, /faq, and /tax-rates for core queries like 'back out tax calculator'. /how-it-works is thin content (391 words).

Recommendation: 301 redirect /how-it-works to /#how-it-works on the homepage, and canonicalize or 301 redirect /faq to /#faq.

Branding & Entity Dilution (PreTaxPrice vs Reverse Sales Tax Calculator) Medium

The domain is salestaxreversecalculator.com, but the UI header and Organization schema declare 'PreTaxPrice'. This fragments entity authority.

Recommendation: Unify the brand identity: 'Reverse Sales Tax Calculator by PreTaxPrice' in schema, OpenGraph, and footer.
