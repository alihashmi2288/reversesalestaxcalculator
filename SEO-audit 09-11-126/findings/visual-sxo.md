# Search Experience Optimization (SXO) & Visual Audit

**Audit Date:** November 9, 2026

## Executive Overview
Search Experience Optimization (SXO) combines SEO visibility with user conversion and task satisfaction. For a financial calculator, searchers expect instant answers above the fold with zero friction.

---

## 1. Above-the-Fold UX Analysis
- **H1 Header:** Clearly visible above the fold on desktop and mobile.
- **Calculator Form:** Positioned immediately below the H1 header. Users can enter a total receipt amount and tax percentage without scrolling.
- **Dynamic Pre-Tax Calculation:** Pre-tax amount, tax paid, and effective rate calculate instantly upon keystroke.
- **Formula Diagram:** Preloaded visual diagram demonstrates the exact arithmetic formula.

---

## 2. Mobile Experience
- **Viewport Meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=5"/>` (PASS).
- **Horizontal Scroll:** Zero horizontal overflow across all tested viewports.
- **Touch Targets:** All calculator buttons, state selector dropdowns, and navigation pills meet minimum 48x48px touch-target standards.
- **Font Legibility:** 16px base font size with high-contrast emerald green theme (`#008254`).
