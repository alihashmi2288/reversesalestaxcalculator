## 2. State Pages Content Analysis
All 50 US state pages provide:
- Exact combined state + local tax rates for 2026.
- Interactive calculation tool tailored to the state rate.
- Formula walkthrough with state-specific examples.
- Local county/city sales tax rate breakdown table.
- State-specific sales tax exemptions (groceries, prescription medicine, clothing).

### Opportunity: Address Repetitive Template Phrasing
While tax rates and county data are customized, the introductory and closing paragraphs share very similar sentence structures across all 50 states.
**Action:** Enhance the top 10 highest-traffic state pages (NY, CT, TX, UT, CA, NC, FL, GA, VA, AR) with custom commentary on unique state tax laws (e.g., California's district taxes, Texas's origin vs destination sourcing, New York's clothing exemption threshold under $110).

## 3. High-Intent Query Word Problem Opportunities
Google Search Console indicates searchers frequently submit full tax calculation word problems:
- *"christa went shopping for back-to-school clothes. before the tax was added on, her total was $176. if the sales tax is 6%, what was the total amount she paid?"* (Pos 9.4, 27 impressions)
- *"how to calculate sales tax backwards from total"* (Pos 9.7, 39 impressions)
- *"formula to back out sales tax"* (Pos 8.0, 22 impressions)

**Action:** Add a "Common Math & Word Problems" FAQ module with instant answers for standard purchase totals ($20, $50, $100, $176) at 4%, 6%, 7%, and 8% tax rates. This directly targets Google AI Overviews and featured snippets.
