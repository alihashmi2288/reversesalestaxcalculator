# Brag Plan: Reverse Sales Tax Calculator

## What is this app?
Reverse Sales Tax Calculator (salestaxreversecalculator.com) is a free, instant, 100% client-side privacy-first financial utility that reverses sales tax out of receipt and invoice totals down to the exact cent, supporting all 50 US states, Canada GST/HST, and European VAT.

## The angle
"The mathematical antidote to receipt guesswork." An Apple-style product film highlighting immaculate typography, instantaneous browser-based calculations, and complete jurisdictional coverage—proving that even a tax utility can feel luxurious, precise, and effortless.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A macro cinematic focus on a crumpled receipt total of **$108.25** dissolving into a sleek, minimalist digital display: *"You have the total. But what was the real price?"*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Reverse Calculation in Motion**: Entering `$108.25`, tapping the `8.25%` chip, and watching the numbers smoothly calculate to reveal `$100.00` original price and `$8.25` tax.
- **Statutory Jurisdictions at a Glance**: Seamlessly cycling across California, Texas, New York, Canada GST/HST, and European VAT.
- **Client-Side Privacy Guarantee**: Highlighting the zero-server, instant in-browser computation with no signup required.

## Outro / punchline
The elegant emerald logo lockup: *"Pre-tax clarity. Instantly free. salestaxreversecalculator.com."*

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: User types receipt total `$108.25`.
2. **Key Action**: Selects `8.25%` combined state/local rate.
3. **Result**: Live three-card breakdown illuminates with the pre-tax price (`$100.00`) highlighted in emerald green.
