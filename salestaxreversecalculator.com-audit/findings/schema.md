## 1. Homepage Structured Data Implementation
The homepage features 7 valid JSON-LD schema blocks:
1. **Organization:** Defines brand entity (`Reverse Sales Tax Calculator by PreTaxPrice`), official URL, and customer support contact point.
2. **WebSite:** Includes search action target for site navigation.
3. **WebApplication:** Specifies application category (`FinanceApplication`), operating system compatibility (`All`), and free offering tier.
4. **HowTo:** Step-by-step procedural markup (`How to Calculate Reverse Sales Tax (Pre-Tax Price)`) with 3 clear steps and formula proofs.
5. **Person:** Outlines CPA / Tax Research Specialist author credentials.
6. **FAQPage:** 6 detailed Q&As enabling accordion rich snippets in Google search results.
7. **ImageObject:** Documents the formula diagram infographic with descriptive metadata.

## 2. State & Hub Structured Data
- State pages feature 4 JSON-LD blocks:
  - `Organization`
  - `BreadcrumbList` (verified valid in Google Search Console URL inspection)
  - `WebApplication`
  - `FAQPage`

## 3. Discrepancy & Opportunity
- **New York State Schema Typo:** The WebApplication schema on `/us/new-york` lists `name: "New York Sales Tax Calculator"` rather than `"New York Reverse Sales Tax Calculator"`.
- **HowTo Schema on State Pages:** Adding localized HowTo steps on the top 10 state pages will increase eligibility for featured calculation snippets.
